## Brand & Style
The design system caters to an elite wholesale and retail imitation jewellery house rooted in heritage, elegance, and tactile richness. It balances the grandeur of royal Indian heirloom craft with high-end contemporary digital luxury. The emotional response sought is prestige, craftsmanship, and tactile delight—reminiscent of inspecting fine Kundan, Polki, and temple jewellery under warm showroom illumination.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Luxury & Layered Glassmorphism**:
- Multi-tiered dimensional depth using translucent frosted wine and gold glass.
- Radiant golden luminescence accentuating jewellery silhouettes and key calls-to-action.
- Realistic multi-layered drop shadows, creating the illusion of floating velvet-lined presentation trays and cut-out jewellery pieces.
- High editorial typography evoking bespoke catalogues and heritage auction houses.
- Strictly prohibited: Any green undertones, tints, or status indicators. Success metrics, badges, and positive alerts must rely exclusively on warm gold, luminous amber, or refined ivory cues.

## Colors
The palette evokes imperial velvet, spun bullion, and polished ivory.

### Hierarchy & Role Allocation
- **Primary Wine Gradient Foundation:** Backgrounds transition seamlessly between `#2A0A14` (Deep Imperial Maroon) and `#6B1E2E` (Regal Rich Wine). It provides high-contrast, velvet-like canvas depth for jewellery displays.
- **Champagne Gold (`#D4AF6A`):** The primary metallic accent, applied to interactive highlights, premium tags, active tab indicators, and luxury borders.
- **Light Gold (`#F0D9A0`):** Used for focal point luminescence, micro-radiance halos behind jewellery cutouts, hover state highlights, and high-visibility monetary values.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FBF6EE`):** The primary text and high-contrast foreground tone, ensuring effortless legibility against deep wine backgrounds.
- **Charcoal Umber (`#26211E`):** Used strictly for high-contrast typography inside gold or ivory-filled buttons, pill tags, and tooltip containers.

### Functional States (Strict No-Green Rule)
- **Success / In-Stock / Verified:** Soft Pale Gold (`#F0D9A0`) paired with subtle Champagne gold ring glows. Never use emerald, mint, or green accents.
- **Warning / Low Stock:** Warm Radiant Amber (`#E29938`).
- **Error / Sold Out:** Ruby Crimson (`#A31D24`).
- **Info / Wholesale Inquiries:** Rose Tinted Ivory (`#E8D4D8`).

## Typography
The typographic voice pairs classical, regal serif headlines (**EB Garamond**) with hyper-legible, geometric sans-serif body and transactional data (**Inter**).

- **EB Garamond** handles editorial statements, collection titles, ornament descriptions, and luxury showcase intros. It conveys the rich heritage of handcrafted jewellery.
- **Inter** guarantees precision in wholesale pricing tiers, order minimums (MOQ), weight specifications, dimensions, SKU labels, and B2B cart calculations.
- Display numbers (such as retail rates vs. bulk discounts) use Inter's tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with semi-bold weights for seamless scanning across stock lists.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects high-end jewellery vitrines: spacious, structured, and unhurried. 

### Grid System
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid with `3rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers cap at a max-width of `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column responsive grid with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Showcase Composition
- Primary wholesale catalogues display an asymmetrical staggered card structure, allowing high-value jewellery cutouts to extend beyond the top and side bounds of glass card backdrops.
- Maintain wide vertical section separation using `space-xl` and above to provide an expansive, museum-grade viewing rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is central to the design system, creating the illusion of heavy velvet stages, suspended jewellery, and frosted bevels.

### The Glassmorphic Surface Tier
- **Level 1 (Ambient Background Canvas):** Angled base gradient: `radial-gradient(ellipse at top, #6B1E2E 0%, #2A0A14 85%)`.
- **Level 2 (Display Trays & Showcase Cards):** Translucent frosted wine glass (`rgba(42, 10, 20, 0.65)`), overlaid with a 1px border styled as `rgba(212, 175, 106, 0.25)` and a dual-blur treatment: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Drawers, Sheets, Modals):** Deeper opacity glass (`rgba(30, 8, 15, 0.88)`), framed with a luminous gold gradient border (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(240, 217, 160, 0.4), rgba(212, 175, 106, 0.1))`).

### Drop Shadow & Illumination Physics
1. **Multi-Layered Drop Shadows:** Applied to floating product cutouts and luxury presentation cards:
   - `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(10, 2, 5, 0.6)` (ambient contact occlusion).
   - `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(10, 2, 5, 0.8)` (depth separation).
   - `0 0 24px 2px rgba(212, 175, 106, 0.12)` (warm gold back-light reflection).
2. **Floating Cut-outs:** High-resolution PNGs of imitation necklaces, bangles, and bridal chokers sit on the z-index tier above their card containers, casting cast-shadows downward onto the frosted glass surface.

## Shapes
The shape profile combines architectural precision with soft, tailored curves. 
- A base radius of `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`) gives structure to standard items, while larger cards and floating showcase stages transition to `rounded-lg` (1rem) and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem) to evoke polished stone, cut gems, and velvet jewelry boxes.
- Floating tags, B2B wholesale MOQ pills, and secondary actions take full pill styling (`rounded-full`) to offer fluid tactile balance against structured product frames.
- Image cut-out pods utilize soft asymmetric arches (e.g., top borders `1.5rem`, bottom borders `0.5rem`) reminiscent of traditional Indian jharokha silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Reserve Wholesale" / "Enquire"):** Rich gradient fill transitioning from Champagne Gold (`#D4AF6A`) to Light Gold (`#F0D9A0`). Text is rendered in deep Charcoal Umber (`#26211E`) with bold Inter typography. Subtly backlit with a `0 4px 18px rgba(212, 175, 106, 0.3)` glow that expands on hover.
- **Secondary ("View Catalog"):** Frosted wine surface (`rgba(107, 30, 46, 0.3)`) with a crisp 1px gold border (`#D4AF6A`). Text is Warm Ivory (`#FBF6EE`). Hover state adds an inner gold glow: `inset 0 0 12px rgba(240, 217, 160, 0.2)`.
- **Text / Inline Actions:** Warm Ivory with a shimmering gold underline that glides inward on hover.

### Floating Product Cards (Showcase Trays)
- Background: Translucent frosted wine panel with 18px backdrop blur and a `1px` stroke tinted in `#D4AF6A` at 20% opacity.
- Top Element: Cut-out jewellery silhouette resting slightly beyond the top boundary, accompanied by a dynamic light-gold bloom behind the metalwork (`filter: drop-shadow(0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.7)) drop-shadow(0 0 20px rgba(240, 217, 160, 0.15))`).
- Bottom Section: Jewel title in EB Garamond, accompanied by B2B bulk slab tiers (e.g., "1–11 pcs" vs "12+ pcs") displayed in tabular Inter body text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Wholesale Tier Pills:** Frosted wine base with gold micro-borders and light gold text.
- **Stock Indicators:** Replaces conventional green with an "Available for Dispatch" badge featuring a tiny 6px glowing champagne-gold dot (`#F0D9A0`) and Warm Ivory text.
- **Authenticity & Polish Grade Badges:** Polished gold-leaf background tint with dark charcoal text.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Deep wine obsidian tone (`rgba(20, 5, 10, 0.7)`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 106, 0.3)`.
- Focus State: Border snaps to solid Light Gold (`#F0D9A0`) with an outer box-shadow halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 106, 0.25)`).
- Placeholder: Soft muted rose-ivory (`rgba(251, 246, 238, 0.45)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Base: Unchecked states exhibit a 1.5px champagne gold outline over transparent wine glass.
- Checked: Filled with Champagne Gold (`#D4AF6A`), bearing an engraved charcoal tick or radio dot. Absolutely no green checkmarks or rings.

### Wholesale Order Slab Matrix (B2B Table Component)
- Layered floating table card with alternating zebra bands using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` and `rgba(107, 30, 46, 0.15)`.
- Header row pinned in frosted dark wine glass with `EB Garamond` column captions.
- Action column with quick quantity increments and instant bulk margin calculations rendered in `#F0D9A0`.
